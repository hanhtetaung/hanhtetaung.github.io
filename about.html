Random Facts

Timeline

  • 2000: The year I was born

    I was born and raised in Dawei, Myanmar, in the southern part of the country. It is a lovely coastal city.

  • 2016: Finished High school

    I completed high school in Yangon, the economic center of Myanmar.

  • 2016: Discovered Programming

    I was first introduced to programming by my cousin, who worked as a software engineer. My first program was a calculator using VBScript.

  • 2017-2020: University of Yangon

    I studied Computer Science at the University of Yangon. Unfortunately, COVID-19, the global pandemic, happened during my third year’s first-semester exams. The university shut down and the exams were suspended.

  • 2021: Software Developer

    I landed my first software development role building a hospital management system through a friend, after creating a website for his studio. The system is still running today.

  • 2022: Moved to Thailand

    Fortunately, my academic journey continued in Thailand. I continued studying Computer Science at Assumption University as a transfer student.

  • 2025: Graduated in Thailand

    I completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Assumption University, Thailand. My favorite subject was Computer Architecture.

  • 2025: Remote Software Developer

    After graduating, I worked remotely as a software developer for a Singapore-based company for six months through a friend's referral.

  • Present: Web design & develop

    Today, I found my passion in web design and development. I continue learning and am open to opportunities in this field. Let’s see how far can go.
